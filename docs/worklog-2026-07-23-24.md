## Part I — Momentum trader: multi-slot + per-token architecture

### The question that drove it

"Most of the time we do nothing." Investigated and answered with data:

- **Forcing one asset to trade more destroys the edge.** JitoSOL 150d activity
  bands: up to ~3–4 trades/week P&L holds or improves; past ~5/week the whole grid
  collapses (best band row: +5.81/+7.90, SQN 0.08). Every extra probe pays ~20 bps
  + gas into noise.
- **Mean-reversion cannot farm the quiet stretches** (on an LST): 2/396 configs
  robust, best +0.65/+0.44 per slice at $100, 82% drawdown. Dead.
- **Conclusion: smoothness = 1/√N stream diversification**, not more events per
  stream. The machinery already existed: multi-token watch list, per-token
  overrides, `MOMENTUM_MAX_POSITIONS`.

**Unit-scale law** (bug class found twice): per-token `min_metric` is denominated
in the GLOBAL metric's units over the GLOBAL lookback — changing
`MOMENTUM_RANK_METRIC`/`LOOKBACK_OBS` silently invalidates every existing
per-token bar (a return-units 0.2353 becomes a near-zero slope_r2 bar). Any global
metric change ⇒ re-derive all per-token bars in the same run.

## Part III — Admiral ship: the raw-RPC path (Phase 1 SHIPPED, commit `1f2ba6c`)

### Why

The arb bot has never landed a Jito bundle. The July-5 investigation had already
ruled out code, tips (lost at 6,108× floor), and concurrency: the causes are
free-feed starvation + phantom stale edges, and fresh simple cycles measured
≈ −8 bps — with real +11–55 bps bursts on quiet paths as the open question.
The user's idea: hold USDC inventory; on a 2-hop edge, send **one raw
transaction** — no Jito.

### The insight that makes it valid

A wallet-funded 2-hop cycle fits in ONE transaction that needs **no address
lookup tables** — and a v0 message with zero lookups is immune by construction to
the `ProgramAccountNotFound` failure on non-Jito validators (the documented reason
raw submission was abandoned). No tip, no auction, landable on 100% of leader
slots. Two design findings mattered:

- **Finding A**: the floor-tip predicate required flash-loan mode; untouched, every
  wallet-funded USDC cycle would be priced with a phantom 51% ratio tip and gated
  to death. Fixed by extending the predicate (regression-locked by test).
- **Finding B**: a failed bundle is free; an included-and-reverted raw tx pays
  base + priority fees (~$0.05 worst case at 600k CU). Fee burn is a first-class
  metric (`est_fee_burn` in the `RAW summary` line).

### What shipped

`ENABLE_RAW_RPC` (default off; non-native base only) + `BASE_BALANCE_RESERVE_UNITS`
(momentum shared-wallet reserve, applied to arb sizing AND the P&L-halt threshold);
`raw_rpc_candidate` + empty-ALT size probe in `build_opportunity`
(`ArbOpportunity.raw_rpc`, `route: raw-rpc` summary marker); `build_raw_wallet_tx`
(one tx, zero lookups asserted, ≤1232 asserted, **no tip tx**);
`RawStats`/`classify_raw_status` (`src/arbitrage/raw_stats.rs` — deliberately
separate from the LATENCY ring); transport branch + `monitor_raw_outcome` in
`main.rs` (own DRY_RUN branch, cooldown parity, 90 s-capped signature polling,
verbatim on-chain error logging; **a raw SEND error never falls back to Jito** —
double-execution risk — only a raw BUILD error does). 190/190 tests, 12 new;
flag-off is byte-identical (test-locked).

### Rollout (NOT yet run) — the experiment that decides everything

1. **DRY_RUN 24 h**: `DRY_RUN=true ENABLE_RAW_RPC=true BASE_MINT=USDC
   INPUT_BASE_UNITS=50000000 MIN_PROFIT_BASE_UNITS=5000 JITO_BUNDLE_THRESHOLD=20`
   (USDC scales are 6dp — unset INPUT_BASE_UNITS means 1,000 USDC). Read:
   `route: raw-rpc` share, `[DRY RUN] would send raw tx` count,
   `raw size-gate fallback` rate (~100% fallback = 2-hop txs don't fit without
   ALTs → stop; test fixtures say shared-market legs fit, fully-distinct Raydium
   pairs don't).
2. **Live probe**: `DRY_RUN=false`, 50 USDC + ≥0.1 SOL gas, momentum stays paper
   (or set the reserve). Success = first landed raw tx ever, zero
   `ProgramAccountNotFound`. That exact error string in `RAW outcome … failed`
   falsifies the hypothesis and ends the experiment.
